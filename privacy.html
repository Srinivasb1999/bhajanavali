ಭಜನಾವಳಿ

Privacy Policy · Effective 31 October 2026

Bhajanavali does not collect, store or share any personal information. This page explains what the app does with data.

What the app loads

Song titles, lyrics, composers, deities and ragas are loaded from our song database, hosted on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). Loading songs does not require an account and does not send any personal information about you.

What stays on your device

  • Your favourite songs
  • Recently viewed songs
  • Your settings: light or dark mode, lyrics text size and lyrics script

These are stored only on your phone and never leave it. Deleting the app removes them.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No advertising
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access

Sharing

When you share a song, the app creates the text or image on your phone and hands it to the share sheet you choose. Nothing is sent to us.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's GitHub Issues page.
